## Demonstrated Defects And Repairs

1. Successful asynchronous work had no service-level `started_at`, and the
   market-session recorder accepted synchronous completion states only. Workers
   now retain an actual start/end interval, and the existing recorder accepts
   `worker_completed` on the exact clean build. Skipped, failed, deferred,
   unfinished, other-build and pending-circuit work still cannot earn credit.
   Historical receipts are not rewritten or assigned guessed start times.
2. The economic evaluator required the retired v2 soak schema while the producer
   emits the stricter minute-availability schema. It now imports the producer's
   canonical schema version. Legacy/unknown schema results do not activate it.
   Fresh broker truth, five-session readiness, real exchange hours, the frozen
   baseline and the reviewed policy/account binding remain required.
3. The seven-date legacy observer had not refreshed since July, although its
   source ledger still receives real owner observations. The existing owner now
   refreshes that existing observer after recording a real cycle. Integration
   probes do not create observations; projection/validation errors remain visible.
   The seven-date contract and version exclusions are unchanged.
4. A successful resend of an expired public payload could complete a publication
   repair without fresh output. Publication repair now includes its existing
   dashboard producer when local output is missing, future-dated or overdue.
   A current send receipt cannot close recovery with an expired/unconfirmed
   payload. The receiver's existing 600-second expiry is shared by the status and
   recovery checks. No freshness threshold is relaxed.
5. The independent Telegram monitor classified the intentional frontier hold as
   a general team failure, unlike the critic. Both now use the same narrow
   advisory classifier. Telegram also requires all ten pipeline stages and zero
   validation errors before treating that limitation as advisory. Other role,
   pipeline, provider and validation failures still alert. Recovery text explicitly
   says Gemini remains held; it does not describe four healthy roles.
6. Live cutover exposed a second publication starvation cause: a batch's order
   could be chosen before an asynchronous projection finished. After a long
   higher-priority job, the producer was restarted before publishing its newly
   completed output. Bounded dispatch now rechecks the shared handoff condition
   at producer admission, yielding a still-fresh unpublished snapshot to the
   existing publication worker. The initial sort and admission check use one
   helper. Closed circuits, selected services, available job budget and actual
   output clocks are required; care/entry priority and resource locks are
   unchanged. Unknown or expiring output is not protected from needed refresh.
7. A final live readback found that Telegram treated the owner's deliberately
   incomplete real observation requirement as an operational failure. The monitor
   now recognises only the exact observation-only limitation on an otherwise
   verified build with passed exposure integrity and no open repairs. Its recovery
   message explicitly says observation remains in progress and unattended
   reliability is not yet certified. Unknown blockers, mismatched builds, stale
   heartbeats, exposure failures and repair requests still alert. No owner flag,
   certification count, execution decision or observation record is changed.

No live fault injection, forced order, extra scheduler, capital increase,
credential change, strategy promotion or database migration was used.
