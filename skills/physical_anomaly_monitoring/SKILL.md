# Physical Anomaly Monitoring

Purpose: interpret physical-world anomalies such as thermal events, maritime disruption, aviation movement, outage patterns, and chokepoint stress.

Inputs:

- NASA FIRMS physical anomaly events.
- Future AIS, Wingbits, GPSJam, IODA, and infrastructure feeds.
- Source heartbeat and trust state.

Outputs:

- Physical anomaly packet.
- Corroboration requirements.
- Market channels that could be affected.

Boundary: physical anomalies are observations, not direct trade triggers.
