# Prediction Markets

Purpose: compare Qadam's event probability estimates with prediction-market pricing and liquidity context.

Inputs:

- Polymarket and Kalshi source status.
- Prediction-market papers and stack references.
- Proposed catalyst packet.

Outputs:

- Probability gap note.
- Liquidity and market-structure caveats.
- Read-only venue readiness flags.

Boundary: this skill can reason about markets, but cannot place prediction-market orders.
