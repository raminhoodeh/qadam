# Risk And Postmortems

Purpose: keep Qadam's risk, execution-readiness, learning, and postmortem loops deterministic and auditable.

Inputs:

- Risk caps and kill-switch state.
- Execution venue status.
- Closed trade outcome records.
- Source and signal quality notes.

Outputs:

- Risk decision note.
- Postmortem packet.
- Learning or policy-change recommendation.

Boundary: recommendations are logged. Policy changes and live-capital authority require explicit future approval.
