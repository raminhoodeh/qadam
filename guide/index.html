Current research agenda

Three Current Hypotheses

Qadam is currently testing three foundational hypotheses. They are falsifiable research questions, not proven claims:

Consumer AI execution Can advances in consumer-accessible AI execution technology enable the discovery of a genuine trading edge?
Quantum pattern recognition Can quantum computers recognise a genuine trading pattern and form a successful trading strategy from backtested data where a matched classical approach cannot?
Akber's investment filter Can hedge fund trader Akber's evaluation expertise - captured in his six-stage investment filter - be operationalised successfully through automated systems?

The Short Version

Qadam is a local-first macro intelligence and governed paper-trading system. An unattended Python operator coordinates source ingestion, pattern research, strategy formation, Akber review, shadow observation, portfolio governance, guarded paper execution, lifecycle polling, learning and public visibility.

Qadam is not a public financial-advice product, a signal channel, or a live-capital trading bot. The dashboard is an explanatory, public-safe, public read-only projection of Qadam's operating state.

Use the dashboard to answer five questions:

  1. What is Qadam observing, and which evidence is usable?
  2. Which relationships and strategies are being tested?
  3. What did Akber's filter and Qadam's decision governance conclude?
  4. Did a conclusion become a guarded Alpaca Paper order or position?
  5. What can Qadam legitimately learn, and may that lesson change future behaviour?

A blocked, held, or empty state often means Qadam's controls are working.

The Core Authority Ladder

These states are related, but they are never interchangeable:

ObservationSomething changed in a source, the world, or a market. It does not mean a trade opportunity has been proven.
Qualified evidenceThe information is timely, relevant, provenance-linked, and safe to compare. It does not mean a repeatable pattern exists.
Graph relationshipEvidence, an entity, a market or an earlier result has been connected into a queryable research path. A graph connection is not independent source quorum, a strategy, or trade authority.
PatternA possible source-price relationship deserves investigation. It does not mean its score is a probability of profit.
Strategy hypothesisQadam has described an expression and invalidation. It does not mean validation or present-market review has passed.
Discovery experiment eligibilityA complete current setup may be considered for a small paper experiment that gathers forward evidence. It is not a validated edge or proof.
Validated edgeThe relationship survived required historical and forward evidence gates. It does not mean it is automatically tradeable now.
Akber pass, hold, or vetoThe current setup is eligible, incomplete, or unsuitable. A pass is not risk approval, a Router decision, or an order.
Governed decisionRouter and portfolio controls produced one current state. It does not mean every positive decision will result in an order.
Paper execution stateA guarded Alpaca Paper lifecycle record exists. It does not enable live capital.
Supported lessonAn attributable outcome supports a cautious conclusion. It does not mean Qadam may silently change a strategy, risk rule, or code.
Applied improvementA tested, reviewed, approved, versioned change entered the next cycle. It does not mean the change is permanently correct or exempt from monitoring and rollback.

Every dashboard page is read-only. It cannot promote research, approve risk, submit paper orders, write to a broker, alter code or policy, award proof credit, or enable live capital.

Stable Operating Model Versus Current State

The stable model is evidence-gated, paper-only, real-calendar, idempotent, and lineage-backed. It does not backfill days, simulate elapsed time, or force trades. The strict validated-strategy lane is separate from a bounded discovery lane that can gather small real paper outcomes without claiming an edge.

An append-only temporal evidence graph gives Qadam persistent experiment memory. Its rebuildable local index connects observations, patterns, tests, rejections, strategy versions, decisions and outcomes, while preserving the difference between observed, inferred, tested and governed evidence.

The frozen risk ladder allows up to US$500 for a first discovery experiment, up to US$2,000 after independent repeat confirmation, and an absolute US$5,000 ceiling only for a validated paper setup. One score or one winning trade cannot advance a tier.

At this edition, graph-assisted discovery is implemented and its five-real-market-day trial counts only actual eligible sessions. Operator health, PaperOps state, Router handoffs and validated-edge state are mutable: read observation_ready, ready_idle and provisional_soak from the timestamped dashboard rather than treating this guide as live telemetry.

Current counts, providers, source freshness, service state, certifications, research maturity, candidates, hardware evidence, and paper-route readiness can change. Read those facts from the current dashboard - especially System Overview, Decision Room, Order Monitor, and page-specific status cards - not from this guide.

Research Pipelines Approaching GateEvidence: active relationships approaching Akber and the maturity entering the filter.

How To Read Quantum Edge

Quantum Edge is an independent proof room for selected nonlinear or quantum-assisted comparisons. It does not claim that every pattern needs quantum computation. Python prepares point-in-time evidence, the strongest fair classical method establishes the benchmark, and specialist methods are compared on the same frozen evidence.

The current conclusion comes from the canonical Quantum Edge projection rather than this guide. Its three principal rows are collapsed by default and should be opened in this order:

Experiment & EvidenceWhat was tested, compared and verified?
Strategy & Paper ImpactDid the result improve a strategy or paper decision?
Quantum Edge VerdictHas a genuine market-level quantum advantage been proven?

The canonical projection keeps provider readiness, local simulation, reproducibility, hardware execution, untouched market comparison, strategy contribution, paper impact, and freshness as separate facts. Provider readiness does not prove hardware execution. A successful simulation does not prove predictive or economic advantage. “Classical preferred” is a valid scientific outcome.

Results & Lessons Versus Tests & Improvements

Results & LessonsWhat happened, and what can Qadam legitimately learn? It attributes outcomes and may produce a supported lesson plus a measurable next test—not changed code or strategy.
Tests & ImprovementsHas that lesson earned the right to change Qadam's behaviour? It tests, observes, reviews, versions, monitors, applies, or rejects one proposed change.

A result is not automatically a lesson, and a sensible lesson is not automatically an improvement. Reference-only broker history remains context rather than Qadam proof. Only a separately approved, timestamped, monitored, reversible version may re-enter Observe.

How Qadam Could Make Money In Paper Trading

The economic hypothesis is that Qadam can find information or structural relationships before market prices fully reflect them, prove that they repeat after realistic costs, and express them through a governed liquid paper instrument. The ten-stage lifecycle is the operating flow.

This is an intended mechanism, not a promise of profit. Any edge must be proven through attributable, out-of-sample, cost-aware paper evidence.

Strict proof lane

Validated strategy

The relationship has survived Qadam's full historical, holdout, cost, robustness and forward evidence standard before current tradeability review.

Bounded evidence lane

Discovery experiment

A complete current hypothesis may collect a small paper outcome before full validation. It still requires a trigger, direction, confirmation, expectancy, invalidation, liquidity, decision-time shadow evidence and lineage.

How Qadam Finds And Tests Edge

An edge is not one headline, one chart, one model opinion, a private prior, a Telegram message, a green label, or a high research score. A Pattern Recognition score ranks evidence for investigation; it is not automatically a calibrated probability, expected return, or chance of profit.

  1. Record a point-in-time observation and only the information available then.
  2. Map it to a watched price or probability without future leakage.
  3. Measure the outcome only after the relevant horizon elapses.
  4. Test repetition after realistic costs and false-discovery controls.
  5. Challenge it with walk-forward and untouched holdout evidence.
  6. Use Quantum Edge only where nonlinear structure could plausibly help.
  7. Observe the frozen idea forward over real time without an order.
  8. Promote, hold, reject, or mark it faded based on the evidence.

Akber's 6-Stage Decision-Making Filter

Akber does not originate an idea, prove a historical edge, approve risk, or execute an order. It asks whether an evidence-classified setup is practical in current market conditions. Validated strategies and bounded discovery setups use declared evidence profiles, but neither may omit its required trigger, confirmation, expectancy, invalidation, liquidity or lineage.

1. ContextDoes the tested relationship fit the affected market, instrument, history, regime, and volatility?
2. CatalystWhat fresh, specific, trusted event could cause repricing now?
3. ConfirmationDo pricing, technical structure, volume, flow, cross-market evidence, and any required nonlinear challenge agree?
4. RiskDoes value remain after costs, is reward-to-risk justified, and what proves the idea wrong?
5. Execution suitabilityIs there a liquid Alpaca Paper expression with acceptable friction and duplicate exposure?
6. Postmortem learningAfter an attributable outcome, did the earlier pass, hold, or veto improve the decision?

An Akber pass creates research eligibility only. Research eligibility is not approval. It does not create a candidate by itself, risk or execution approval, a PaperOps handoff, an order, a broker write, proof credit, or live-capital authority.

Paper Evaluation Rules

  • Preserve every declared real-calendar evaluation window.
  • Do not backfill calendar days, simulate elapsed time, or force trades.
  • Keep mirror-only historical broker records separate from proof of Qadam's decision quality.
  • Record the no-trade rationale when no setup qualifies.
  • Keep drawdown, concentration, duplication, spread, liquidity, freshness, and idempotency controls active.
  • Require complete lineage and a postmortem before a closed outcome can enter the paper proof ledger.
  • Keep live capital outside the paper-only route.

Data Source Rules

  • One source is rarely enough; freshness, provenance, and point-in-time safety matter.
  • Private worldview priors are context, not evidence.
  • Supplemental providers remain context until registry and trust rules say otherwise.
  • Broker receipts and portfolio state are execution evidence, not independent market corroboration.
  • A connected provider does not prove complete history or current monitoring.
  • Secrets, identifiers, raw payloads, and local paths must not enter public artifacts.

Red Flags

  • A stale or hard-coded count is presented as current runtime truth.
  • A source claims health without freshness or monitoring evidence.
  • A research score is presented as guaranteed profit.
  • A pattern is called a validated edge without the required evidence.
  • An Akber pass, candidate, Router review, or PaperOps handoff is presented as an order.
  • Reference-only broker history is used to claim Qadam performance.
  • A supported lesson changes Qadam without testing, approval, versioning, monitoring, and rollback.
  • Telegram creates or accepts a command.
  • Any interface claims shell, deployment, broker-write, proof-credit, or live-capital authority.
  • A secret, token, member identifier, raw payload, credential, or local path appears publicly.

A Successful Reading Of Qadam

A new reader should be able to distinguish observation from evidence, pattern from edge, hypothesis from decision, handoff from order, outcome from lesson, and lesson from approved improvement. They should also know which dashboard page contains the current answer and whether the infrastructure is healthy enough to trust it.

Qadam should feel understandable before it feels powerful.
